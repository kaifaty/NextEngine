## Execution rules

1. Each stage receives a bounded contract before implementation and records
   its exact first failure.
2. Formula, discretization, solver convergence, physical validity,
   determinism and performance are separate claims.
3. Long or product-scale runs are forbidden until their tiny preflights pass.
4. Parameters are selected by declared metrics, not by visual tuning.
5. The corrected CPU solver is the research authority through FCR4. CUDA is
   only a mirror until FCR5 and remains report-only through FCR7.
6. Windows remains out of scope for this research lineage. Any later product
   promotion must independently close the platform obligations then in force.
7. No public continuum schema, PhysX coupling, save mutation or gameplay
   publication is authorized by this roadmap.
