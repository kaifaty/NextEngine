## Frozen scope

- **Profile (contracts).** The B0 validation admits 2D single-layer
  textures in `Rgba8Unorm`, `Rg8Unorm` or `R8Unorm` with `Srgb` or
  `Linear` colour space and any mip count the record's chain rule
  allows; materials with one to three bindings: `BaseColor` at uv set
  `0` first, then at most one `MetallicRoughness` and one `Normal`, each
  at uv set `0`; every binding's UV transform is the identity except a
  uniform scale (`x` equal `y`, no offset, no rotation) shared by the
  material's bindings; `Normal` and `MetallicRoughness` textures must be
  `Linear`. The metallic-roughness map follows glTF: `G` roughness, `B`
  metallic, multiplied by the factors.
- **Authoring (project).** A `texture-png` record: `asset_id`,
  `record_revision`, `relative_path` (a declared referenced source of the
  project, so its bytes and license ride the composition lock),
  `color_space`, `alpha`, `mip_levels` (`full` or `none`). The PNG
  decoder (8-bit grey, grey-alpha, RGB and RGBA, the five filters, no
  interlace) expands to `RGBA8`; `full` builds the chain by a `2 x 2`
  box filter, averaging sRGB textures in linear light. The `material`
  record gains optional `metallic_roughness_texture_asset_id`,
  `normal_texture_asset_id` and `uv_scale` (default `1`).
- **Adapter.** Textures upload every mip in the format their encoding
  and colour space name; the texture sampler becomes trilinear with
  repeat addressing. Set 1 grows to three bindings (base colour,
  metallic-roughness, normal): one set per material revision and one per
  base-colour texture revision (the rings and the fallback), flat `1 x 1`
  placeholders where a material has no map (white, `(0, 1, 0)`,
  `(0.5, 0.5, 1)`). The world programs (`b0_textured`,
  `b0_textured_no_shadow`, `b0_reflect`) read the maps: roughness and
  metallic from the map times the factors, the normal from the map
  through the screen-space cotangent frame, the UV scale in the push
  block's spare lane. `MATERIAL_MAPS active materials=N normal=M
  metallic_roughness=K mips=L` printed once.
- **Contract.** `b0.v6` (set 1 bindings 1 and 2, the push block's
  material lane meaning); the roots move and are re-pinned (recorded).
- **Content.** Procedural `512 x 512` PNG sets generated by a checked-in
  script (`tools/textures/generate.py`: ground, concrete, pad), declared
  as referenced sources; the base, works and pad materials bind their
  albedo, normal and metallic-roughness maps with a uniform UV scale
  (`0.5` per metre for the ground); the works mesh `0xe5` gains
  box-projected UVs in metres (its UVs were a constant).
- **Not in scope (recorded).** Block compression (BC5/BC7, no encoder in
  the workspace), anisotropic filtering (the device feature is not
  enabled), a tangent stream (the cotangent frame stands in), occlusion
  and emissive maps, the glTF importer (the next plan of item 5).

## Result (2026-09-05)

Implementation: the B0 profile admits 8-bit 2D textures in sRGB or linear
space with any mip chain and one-to-three bindings under one uniform UV
scale (`catalog.rs`, `UvTransformV1::uniform_scale_q16_16`); the
`texture-png` authoring record with the PNG decoder and the box-filter mip
chain (`authoring/png.rs`), the material's optional maps and `uv_scale`;
the adapter's per-format mip uploads, the trilinear repeat sampler, the
material and base-texture sets with flat placeholders, the maps in the
three world programs through the screen-space cotangent frame, the
contract at `b0.v6` (`860a946d909b…`); the generator
`tools/textures/generate.py` and three `256 x 256` sets declared as
referenced sources (their bytes and license in the composition lock);
the ground, a new concrete material (`0x49`, the works, the pond, the rim
and the crate) and the pads bound to their maps; the works mesh with
box-projected UVs in metres.

Two findings during development, both recorded: nine `512 x 512` sets
with mips exceeded the catalog's `8 MiB` canonical field limit (`12.6 MB`
of texels), so the sets are `256 x 256` (the limit stands as an open item
for the glTF importer); and `project run` on the reference project fails
independently of this plan (`BootstrapClosureMismatch` at the
world-routine principal for a single-tick run), which the content-package
check never exercises (it runs the creator-smoke project); recorded, not
touched.
