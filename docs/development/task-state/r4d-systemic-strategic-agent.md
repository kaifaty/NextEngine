## Initial product decision

### D-001 — One bounded systemic scenario

- **Observation:** The roadmap requires a concrete NPC with no food or currency to learn about work, accept it, travel, work, earn, trade and eat, including threat and typed failure branches.
- **Evidence:** SPEC-32, ADR-020/021/056/073 and the R4 completion criteria in `docs/roadmap.md`.
- **Decision:** Implement exactly that authored scenario and its 100-NPC cadence evidence. Broader bargaining, taxes, crime, factions and macro-economy remain out of scope.
- **Rejected alternatives:** A generic jobs market, simulated outcomes without ordinary owners, or test-only social/economy fixtures.
- **Consequences:** Every visible outcome must correspond to an accepted Agent/Memory proposal and an ordinary World Services or RPG commit.
- **Uncertainty:** The exact current-only schema names and operation count remain under contract freeze until the production command sequence is compiled end to end.
- **Reconsider when:** A second accepted production scenario demonstrates a reusable abstraction instead of a one-consumer contract.

### D-002 — Preserve owner boundaries

- **Observation:** Agent Runtime owns goals/plans/private tasks, Memory owns beliefs/recollections, World Services owns location/tier/job/activity/time and RPG owns resources, inventory, relationships and commitments.
- **Evidence:** ADR-020, ADR-021, ADR-056 and ADR-073.
- **Decision:** Extend the accepted R4c proposal boundary. Structured acts carry bounded semantic payloads; commitment, currency and inventory facts become true only after RPG commit, while job/activity and bulk-time transitions become true only after World Services commit.
- **Rejected alternatives:** A monolithic NPC snapshot, direct executive mutation, or reuse of quest/dialogue state as an implicit job commitment.
- **Consequences:** Save/replay must include every new authoritative owner segment and its ledger receipt.
- **Uncertainty:** Whether job/activity can remain a narrow extension of the existing population owner or needs a distinct World Services owner is decided by the exact mutation and cadence consumer.
- **Reconsider when:** Owner validation cannot reject stale/no-job/no-money/no-route branches without cross-owner writes.

### D-003 — Current-only contracts follow the consumer

- **Observation:** Alpha persistence has no public predecessor and ADR-046 forbids speculative compatibility work.
- **Evidence:** ADR-046 and current authoring V5 / activation V6 / Replay V8.
- **Decision:** Add only schema fields and operations consumed by the R4d production path, then advance current-only authoring/activation/replay versions once exact persistence breadth is known.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Optional unused schemas, migration scaffolding or legacy format acceptance.
- **Consequences:** Older alpha encodings fail typed unsupported; no migration claim is made.
- **Uncertainty:** Final successor version numbers depend on whether R4d adds one or more owner segments.
- **Reconsider when:** A public v1 persisted predecessor exists.
