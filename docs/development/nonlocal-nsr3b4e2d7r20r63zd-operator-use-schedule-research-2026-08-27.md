## Question

operator is sufficient to lose the state-2 certificate on the frozen


without changing the operator values, input, factor, precision, update count

## Minimal discriminator

Enumerate the complete three-bit schedule. Each bit independently selects the
tangent or common product for `P0`, `P1` and `P2`; all eight schedules use the
original binary128 RHS and inverse scale.

The endpoints are already known:

- `TTT` must reproduce R63X/R63ZC tangent-original and pass state 2;
- `CCC` must reproduce R63ZC common-original and reject every state.

No monotonicity is assumed. The result is the full eight-bit ladder mask plus
all inclusion-minimal sets of common substitutions that reject state 2. This
handles non-monotone and interacting schedules without selecting one favored
slot or fitting a numerical threshold.

the first causal application or interaction.
