## Frozen boundary

The contract has three deliberately different states:

1. `prospective_catalogue_eligible`: primary-source project/revision, stable
   physical parent, at least one runtime descriptor and publisher-hashed
   waveform/transfer correspondence;
2. `corpus_materialization_eligible`: selected payload bytes are fetched,
   verified and structurally parsed, record-to-parent and target extraction are
   frozen, and a disclosed role plan is immutable;
3. `psel_pack_eligible`: materialized evidence from at least two independent
   publisher/project revisions supports a signal-blind material-pack choice.

Passing an earlier state grants no later authority. Repetitions are records,
not new physical parents. Mirrors, derivatives, re-encodes and shared physical
objects co-locate in one source component.

## Wider source frontier

The research pass also checked current primary-source status without granting
catalogue credit:

- [REALIMPACT](https://samuelpclarke.com/realimpact/) reports 50 real objects,
  geometry, impact/listener positions, force profiles and recordings. Its
  physical objects are part of the already disclosed
  REALIMPACT/ObjectFolder/AV-MSF component, so a full 50-object roster/alias
  bridge is required before any exact *new-parent* count;
- [AV-MSF](https://zisenshao.github.io/AV-MSF/) still labels its code as
  coming soon. The ten existing page-card objects are already in C0R and do not
  constitute a fresh project;
- [FillGauss/FillImpact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17773) describes more than
  5,000 recordings over 88 fill-conditioned objects, but the reviewed paper
  page exposes no official released data manifest yet;
- [VibraVerse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20422) promises a large synthetic
  geometry/material corpus, but the paper says the dataset will be
  open-sourced and does not authoritatively bind the discovered mirror;
- [NISR](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BumsooKim00/nisr-dataset) is an
  ObjectFolder-derived synthetic teacher candidate, not an independent real
  source project;
- the [first DAFx challenge](https://github.com/LOGUNIVPM/1st-DAFx-Challenge)
  is useful as a synthetic plate-observation benchmark, but secret ground truth
  and synthetic lineage cannot supply real descriptor-to-signal admission.

These are research leads, not source-power rows. G0B1 should not inflate power
from announced releases, mirrors or derivative projects.
