## Research interpretation

Prior work supports the diagnostic direction without deciding the Next Engine
gate:

- [Raghuvanshi, Lloyd and Govindaraju (I3D 2011)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/sound-synthesis-impact-sounds-video-games/)
  reports that direct modal envelopes plus a residual outperform forcing every
  mode into a standard exponential envelope for many game impacts.
- [McDermott and Simoncelli (Neuron 2011)](https://mcdermottlab.mit.edu/papers/McDermott_Simoncelli_2011_sound_texture_synthesis.pdf)
  shows that marginal subband statistics alone are insufficient for realistic
  stochastic sound; modulation and cross-channel correlations carry separate
  structure.
- [Mirabilii, Schlecht and Habets (JASA 2021)](https://www.audiolabs-erlangen.de/resources/2020-JASA-CCR)
  demonstrates that multichannel noise with a target spatial coherence can be
  generated by decomposing a coherence matrix, while spectral smoothness of
  that mixing matters.

The evidence supports a narrow next candidate: preserve the two-exponential
amplitude path and replace only rectangular white excitation with a frozen,
deterministic within-band spectral-color representation. The rank-one spatial
defect remains a recorded follow-up; it must not be silently folded into the
same cycle.
