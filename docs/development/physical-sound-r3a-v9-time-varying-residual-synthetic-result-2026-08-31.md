## Result

deterministic `96`-band residual renderer, `8`-value contact latent, packed
record, neural held-contact field and cost accounting all repeat. All real,
prior-V8-development, sealed, method-holdout and admission-shadow access
counters remain zero.

This proves that V9 can represent and interpolate **known** time-varying
spectro-temporal truth under the experiment budgets. It does not prove that the
same `96 bands / 8 atoms / 128-sample hop` representation is sufficient for
real glass, that real coordinates are available, or that R3B/validator/runtime
work is authorized.
