## Capability boundary

C0 receives a dedicated canonical structural profile. It contains only fresh
material/geometry/contact identities, P1 fixture metadata, role algebra,
witness thresholds, local/gate support equations, resources and the F0
commitment roots. It contains no oracle expression, truth coefficient, target,
training policy, model topology, optimizer, prediction, weight or metric value.

The C0 owner never loads the full F0 scientific profile. It may hash-check the
F0 result and owner, invoke the immutable P1 modal owner and reuse the frozen
target-free V35 census equations. Its AST and recursive profile audit reject
model/tournament/truth imports and forbidden value-bearing sections.

## Required census

For every one of the fresh `1,512` cases and `15,120` modal rows, C0 must:

1. reproduce the F0 case and row commitment roots exactly;
2. solve the P1 fixture and verify finite positive frequency/decay, exact modal
   order, signed-gain factorization and remesh identity;
3. establish per-role/stratum nodal, pickup-sign, non-silent,
   material-sensitive and contact-sensitive witnesses;
4. establish exactly `216` compatible train rows per evaluation row and `215`
   after complete train case-group leave-out;
5. prove finite-positive Gaussian denominators, finite 15-field local keys,
   empty forbidden-field intersection and deterministic contact/geometry
   bandwidths;
6. prove every evaluation row is inside the unchanged strict OOD radius and
   gives both local and neural experts strictly positive weight.

Only counts, canonical identity commitments and finite binary64 ranges may be
published. No local prediction is computed because that would require targets.
