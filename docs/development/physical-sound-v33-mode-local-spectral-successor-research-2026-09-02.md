## Question

Why did a compact physics-locked MLP learn decay and object-global gain but fail
to beat local retrieval for contact correction, and what is the smallest fresh
experiment that can distinguish a representation defect from a capacity or
optimization defect?

This is a bounded research escalation after repeated coordinate-field failures.
It does not reopen the spent V32 development role and cannot select a nearby
seed, width, loss, step count or threshold for the rejected M1 family.

## Exact observation

M1 passed every hard causal/resource gate and 13 of 14 development metric
gates. Its candidate contact RMSE was `0.002283807`, while nearest-train-row was
`0.002053452`; the required ratio was `<=0.90x` and the observed ratio was
`1.112179x`. The same candidate beat ridge by `0.073138x`, and its decay and
global-gain branches beat both ridge and nearest.

The failure clusters at one low-dimensional spatial branch. It is therefore
not evidence that the modal owner, bounded composition, optimizer execution or
the other two residual branches are generally broken. It is also not evidence
that a larger network would help.

## Primary prior art inspected

1. Tancik et al., [Fourier Features Let Networks Learn High Frequency
   Functions in Low Dimensional Domains](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/55053683268957697aa39fba6f231c68-Paper.pdf),
   NeurIPS 2020. The paper identifies spectral bias in ordinary coordinate
   MLPs and shows that a fixed Fourier input map changes the effective kernel
   so higher spatial frequencies can be learned. The bounded claim used here
   is only that a deterministic spectral lift is a justified discriminator for
   the failed raw-coordinate contact head.
2. Sitzmann et al., [Implicit Neural Representations with Periodic Activation
   Functions](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/53c04118df112c13a8c34b38343b9c10-Abstract.html),
   NeurIPS 2020. SIREN demonstrates that periodic activations can represent
   detailed signals and derivatives. It supports the representation
   hypothesis, but its special activation and initialization add a second
   causal change; V33 therefore keeps it as a rejected first-step alternative.
3. Jin et al., [NeuralSound: Learning-based Modal Sound Synthesis with Acoustic
   Transfer](https://www.graphics.pku.edu.cn/docs/20220703145543530591.pdf),
   ACM TOG/SIGGRAPH 2022. NeuralSound couples learning to a modal vibration
   solver and predicts acoustic transfer from surface vibration. The bounded
   lesson is architectural: geometry-conditioned ML can surround modal physics
   without replacing modal identity. Its sparse 3D/radiation networks are far
   beyond the smallest current question and are not adopted.
4. Jin et al., [DiffSound: Differentiable Modal Sound Rendering and Inverse
   Rendering for Diverse Inference Tasks](https://hellojxt.github.io/DiffSound/),
   SIGGRAPH 2024. DiffSound keeps an explicit modal pipeline and makes shape,
   physical attributes and impact position differentiable. It supports keeping
   P1 modes and contact participation explicit. It does not establish that
   V33's synthetic oracle predicts real Steel or Glass.

No source code, model, dataset, checkpoint or audio was downloaded or executed.

## Decision

Select one `mode-local-spectral-residual-v1` candidate. It keeps P1 frequency,
mode order, contact zeros, signs, impulse scaling and remesh identity unchanged;
it also keeps the V32 decay/global-gain decomposition. Only the contact
representation changes:

```text
raw branch-local M1 features
  + fixed axis-aligned sin/cos surface coordinates at preregistered low orders
  + deterministic P1 mode-field samples on one preregistered local stencil
  -> one small bounded contact MLP
```

The exact basis orders, stencil, normalization, topology, seed, optimizer and
resource envelope must freeze in F0 before any new oracle row is materialized.
No random learned positional embedding, object ID, role label, mesh resolution,
waveform decoder, pretrained representation or runtime inference is allowed.

The same fresh corpus must evaluate five controls: P1 identity, nearest train
row, raw-feature ridge, spectral-feature ridge and the unchanged raw-coordinate
MLP topology. This separates `physics`, `local retrieval`, `fixed basis`,
`representation` and `neural nonlinearity` rather than comparing only one new
model with an easy baseline.

## Fresh-evidence rule

The V32 oracle and development values are disclosed. Reusing only new geometry
cells under the same disclosed formula would invite benchmark-specific feature
selection. V33 therefore requires:

- new synthetic material constants, geometry multipliers and contact sets;
- a new multi-component contact truth bank frozen before candidate execution;
- three reported development and holdout strata: unseen geometry with known
  contacts, unseen contacts on known geometry, and both unseen together;
- no M1 row, coefficient, prediction, weight, optimizer state or threshold in
  candidate selection;
- one development opening and, only after a complete pass, one method-holdout
  opening with A/B repeat evidence.

The truth bank may use deterministic P1 modal fields and declared analytic
surface functions, but cannot be an exact copy of the V32 contact formula. Its
coefficients and role membership bind in the F0 profile, not in implementation
code selected after results.

## Falsification and stop rule

Development must show that the selected candidate beats the unchanged raw MLP,
nearest retrieval and spectral ridge on the aggregate contact metric, with no
regression in decay/global gain or any physics-locked gate. Each of the three
generalization strata must remain inside its preregistered absolute and
relative bound. F0 will freeze the exact numbers before values.

If fixed spectral ridge wins but the neural candidate does not, the
representation hypothesis survives but this ML family closes. If neither
spectral method beats the raw/nearest controls, H1/H2 are rejected for this
domain and V33 stops before holdout. If development passes but holdout fails,
the family closes without another basis/order/stencil retry. In every case P1
and authored clips remain valid fallback behavior.

Freeze the V33 F0 corpus/truth commitment, exact spectral/stencil mechanics,
candidate/control identities, access order, metrics and resource bounds without
