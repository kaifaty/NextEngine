## Frozen contact method

The candidate is `coverage-gated-geometry-contact-hybrid-v1`:

1. P1 supplies immutable modal frequency/order, contact participation, local
   stencil, support, pickup sign, impulse scaling and remesh identity.
2. The neural contact expert appends three dimensionless log-geometry fields to
   the V34 32-field spectral/local input. Its two `16`-unit hidden layers and
   bounded output remain compact; decay and global-gain heads are unchanged.
3. The local expert is a Gaussian weighted mean over every train row in the
   exact family/support/mode-ordinal partition. Distance uses only normalized
   material, geometry, contact, frequency and P1 modal fields. Canonical sorting
   before float64 accumulation makes it train-order invariant.
4. Train-time local estimates exclude the complete case/remesh group containing
   the query. Development and holdout use train rows only. There is no self or
   evaluation-role retrieval.
5. The local blend weight is a capped continuous Gaussian of nearest geometry
   and contact support distance. Bandwidths are median positive nearest-neighbor
   distances computed from train coordinates without targets. Exact matches use
   the same equation and never trigger a separate branch.
6. A separately frozen continuous support radius determines OOD rejection. It
   is not a role/stratum detector and cannot change gameplay state; rejection
   selects the authored clip in any future consumer.

The candidate output is the bounded blend of local and neural contact experts.
The `0.80` local-weight cap keeps both experts structurally reachable even on
train support. The local estimator itself is also a binding non-neural control.
